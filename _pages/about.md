I build software and AI products. Right now I'm building [Phrasel](https://www.phrasel.com/), a SaaS platform for language learning and test preparation, where I lead technical development from architecture through deployment and scaling.

### Timeline

<span class="timeline-date">2024 – Now</span> — Co-founder and technical lead at [Phrasel](https://www.phrasel.com/).

<span class="timeline-date">2022 – 2024</span> — AI engineer at Aethos Labs, building machine learning solutions for sales. Work included data preparation and NLP models for prospect engagement prediction, sentiment analysis, deduplication, and bot detection.

<span class="timeline-date">2021 – 2022</span> — SharpestMinds mentorship: built an NLP application for researchers (summarization, classification of scientific articles, and title generation from abstracts).

<span class="timeline-date">2017 – 2020</span> — PhD work at Polytechnique Montréal on algorithms for highly nonlinear structures that deform under fluid flow, advised by [Eric Laurendeau](https://www.polymtl.ca/expertises/en/laurendeau-eric) and [Frederick Gosselin](https://www.polymtl.ca/expertises/en/gosselin-frederick). Findings are in [Flutter Instability of a Reconfiguring Beam with Large Displacement](https://publications.polymtl.ca/5256/). I left the PhD to join the tech industry.

<span class="timeline-date">2013 – 2016</span> — MSc in Aerospace Engineering at Sharif University of Technology with [Masoud Darbandi](http://ae.sharif.edu/~portal/faculty/1097293711), simulating wind-turbine aerodynamics.

<span class="timeline-date">2009 – 2013</span> — BSc in Aerospace Engineering at Sharif University of Technology; undergraduate research on combustion modeling for industrial incinerators.
