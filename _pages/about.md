I build software and AI products. Right now I'm co-founding [Phrasel](https://www.phrasel.com/), a SaaS platform for language learning and test preparation, where I lead technical development from architecture through deployment and scaling.

### Timeline

**2024 – Now** — Co-founder & technical lead at [Phrasel](https://www.phrasel.com/).

**2022 – 2024** — AI engineer at Aethos Labs, building machine learning solutions for sales. Work included data preparation and NLP models for prospect engagement prediction, sentiment analysis, deduplication, and bot detection.

**2021 – 2022** — SharpestMinds mentorship: built an NLP application for researchers (summarization, classification of scientific articles, and title generation from abstracts).

**2017 – 2020** — PhD work at Polytechnique Montréal on algorithms for highly nonlinear structures that deform under fluid flow, advised by [Eric Laurendeau](https://www.polymtl.ca/expertises/en/laurendeau-eric) and [Frederick Gosselin](https://www.polymtl.ca/expertises/en/gosselin-frederick). Findings are in [Flutter Instability of a Reconfiguring Beam with Large Displacement](https://publications.polymtl.ca/5256/). I left the PhD to join the tech industry.

**2013 – 2016** — MSc in Aerospace Engineering at Sharif University of Technology with [Masoud Darbandi](http://ae.sharif.edu/~portal/faculty/1097293711), simulating wind-turbine aerodynamics.

**2009 – 2013** — BSc in Aerospace Engineering at Sharif University of Technology; undergraduate research on combustion modeling for industrial incinerators.
