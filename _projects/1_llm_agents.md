It's sort of surreal to see an AI agent that decides to act on itself to reach some goals! I believe that it's the most exciting avenue for large language models, to act as the brain of a system that has a set of tools at its disposal. I am going to experiment with this idea [here](https://github.com/m-tari/llm-langchain-experiments).
