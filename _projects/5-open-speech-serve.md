Serving speech models well is harder than running a single transcription. [open-speech-serve](https://github.com/m-tari/open-speech-serve) is a toolkit for benchmarking Whisper across HF Transformers, faster-whisper, vLLM, SGLang, and TensorRT-LLM/Triton, with support for load testing, concurrent inference, and measuring streaming latency (TTFS).
